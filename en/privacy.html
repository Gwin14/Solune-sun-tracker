Privacy Policy

Solune · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Summary

Solune has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The developer does not collect, store or sell personal data. Almost everything the app does is calculated on your device.

Location

Your location is used on your device to calculate sun and moon times, positions and reminders. It is never sent to the developer. When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Apple’s geocoding service (part of iOS) to find the place.

Camera and motion sensors

The camera and motion sensors are used only while you are in the AR sky view, to draw the sun’s path over the live image. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Solune Pro is sold through Apple’s App Store. Payments are handled by Apple; the developer never sees your payment details. Purchase status is verified through RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history so your subscription works and can be restored. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Planetarium object pages

When you open a star’s detail page, the app downloads a public summary from Wikipedia. Wikipedia receives a normal web request (IP address and the article requested) under the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Data on your device

Your saved places, journal, favorites and settings stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Solune is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Gwin14/Sun-seeker/issues.
