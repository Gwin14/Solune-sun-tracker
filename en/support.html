Solune · Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? We read every message.

Open a ticket

Contact

Open a ticket on GitHub. You can write in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French or German.

Restore purchases

Open Settings in Solune, tap Solune Pro, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Solune.

Times look wrong?

Check that the right place is selected (Settings › Place) and that location access is allowed. Calculations are accurate to about one minute.

AR is misaligned

Move the phone in a figure-eight to calibrate the compass, or tap the sun on screen to align it manually.
